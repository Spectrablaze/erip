7. Known limitations

AreaLimitationMoves the valueWhat would fix it
Terminal valueThe terminal value is 74.8% of enterprise value in the base case. Return on new capital in perpetuity is set at 12.0%, an analyst decision anchored on the company's own ROIC, not evidence about returns on future berths. Because 12.0% sits close to the 11.5% WACC, terminal growth adds little value (INR 114 to 117 across 3% to 5% growth).yesEvidence on incremental returns from commissioned Phase I assets, once two years of their operating data exist.
Concession lifeThe perpetuity assumes the port concessions are renewed. Concession end dates and renewal terms are not modelled asset by asset.yesA finite-life valuation per concession with a renewal probability.
Working capital historyHistorical working capital comes from Screener balance-sheet movements, which include capital creditors, deposits and balances consolidated with the Navkar acquisition. Reconstructed FY26 operating cash flow misses the reported INR 2,021.5 Cr by INR 1,071 Cr. The forecast inherits a INR 489 Cr working-capital release in FY27, worth about INR 1.5 a share.yesRebuild trade working capital from the annual report notes for FY24 to FY26 and drop Screener's other assets and liabilities from the driver base.
Historical per-share dataShare counts before FY23 are not adjusted for the FY23 capital restructuring (6.07 Cr shares in FY22 to 186.5 Cr in FY23), so historical EPS before FY23 is not comparable with later years. The valuation uses the post-QIP count of 232.35 Cr and is unaffected.noRestate pre-FY23 shares on the post-restructuring basis from the DRHP.
Debt covenantsLoan covenants are not disclosed; the annual report says only that they are complied with. Stress tests measure leverage against Fitch's 3.5x net debt/EBITDA reference, which is not a covenant.noCovenant terms from the bond offering circular or loan agreements, if obtainable.
Minority interestMinority interest is deducted at book value (INR 815 Cr), not at the market value of the minority stakes.yesValue the subsidiaries with minority holders separately and deduct the minority share of that value.
CitationsThree web citations carry no URL, and two cite documents that are not among the hashed inputs. They are graded EXTERNAL and NOT_HELD, not verified.noAdd the URLs and the documents to inputs/, then rerun citation_check.py.
IndependenceOne analyst built the model and wrote the report. No one has rebuilt the company independently, so model risk shared by every check here (a wrong reading of a disclosure, a missed liability) is not tested.yesAn independent rebuild of historical inputs, forecast drivers, WACC and the terminal value, with disagreements documented.
Track recordThe price ledger scores the originally published call (v1, SELL 165). The standing call (v4, SELL 130) has not been republished at a fresh date, so it is not yet in the ledger. Two calls cannot establish skill either way.noOpen a ledger entry whenever a call is published, and score at 12 months.
